Hilora Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the Hilora team ("we", "us" or "our") collects, uses, shares and protects personal data when you use the Hilora mobile application and related services (together, the "Service"). It also explains your rights and how to exercise them. Please read it together with our Terms of Use.

1. Data controller and contact

The Hilora team is the data controller responsible for your personal data under the EU and UK General Data Protection Regulation ("GDPR") and the Turkish Personal Data Protection Law No. 6698 ("KVKK"). The data controller is Kadir Fayzi, Kurtuluş Mahallesi, Güneşli Sokak, Adapazarı/Sakarya, Türkiye. For any question about this policy or your data, contact us at kadir.fayzi@gmail.com.

2. Summary

  • You can use Hilora without giving your name, email address or phone number. The app creates an anonymous account for you. This version offers no sign-in, so your readings stay with that account on this installation.
  • If you use the coffee reading, the photos of your cup and saucer are read by the artificial intelligence and are not stored. Only the text of the reading is kept.
  • We use your birth details to calculate your birth chart. The artificial intelligence that writes readings receives calculated chart data, never your name, birth date or birth place.
  • The daily horoscope is not about you: it is written once for each zodiac sign and language from the positions of the planets that day, so the artificial intelligence receives nothing about you, and everyone who reads that sign in that language sees the same text.
  • Usage statistics and crash reports are collected only if you agree, and you can change your choice at any time in Settings.
  • Free users see ads: a short rewarded video that opens a reading, and banners on a few calm screens. They are served by Google AdMob, only after you answer Google's consent message where the law requires one. Premium users see no ads. Your questions, readings, name and birth details are never used for ads.
  • We do not sell your personal data.
  • You can delete any reading or report, and delete your account and all its data in Settings → Delete account.

3. Personal data we collect

3.1 Data you give us

  • Profile: an optional display name, your main area of interest (for example love or career), and your confirmation that you are at least 18 years old.
  • Birth details: birth date, optional birth time, birth place with its coordinates and time zone.
  • Questions and readings: the questions you ask, the tarot cards drawn for you, follow-up questions, and the readings, daily guidance and reports written for you.
  • Coffee reading photos and text: if you use the coffee reading, the two or three photos of your coffee cup and saucer that you take with the camera or choose from your photo library, and the optional topic and question you add. The app resizes the photos on your device and removes their metadata (such as location and time) before sending them. The photos are sent to our server and on to Google's Gemini service only to be read; they are processed in memory and are never stored or logged by us. The text of the reading is kept like your other readings.
  • Reports: if you report a reading, daily guidance or report as offensive, harmful or wrong, the report: which content it is (its identifier), the reason you choose and the optional note you write. It helps us improve what the artificial intelligence writes.
  • Another person's details: if you create a compatibility report, the details you enter about the other person (an optional name, birth date, whether the birth time is known, and birth place). Enter them only if you are entitled to do so.
  • Messages to us: what you write when you contact us, and your email address.

3.2 Data created when you use the Service

  • Account identifier: a random identifier created for your anonymous account.
  • Calculated chart: the planetary positions, houses and aspects calculated from your birth details.
  • Purchases: the subscription or product you bought, the store (Google Play), dates, status, store transaction identifiers, and the price, currency and country reported by the store. We never receive your payment card details.
  • Usage counters and request records: how many readings you have used and when requests were made, without their content, to apply free limits and protect the Service from abuse.
  • Technical data: when our servers handle a request they record its time, outcome and duration; they do not record your questions, readings or birth details.

3.3 Data collected only with your consent

  • Usage statistics: which features are used (for example "reading completed" or "paywall opened"), with the device model, operating system, app version, approximate country and an app instance identifier, through Google Firebase Analytics.
  • Crash reports: technical details of app errors (stack trace, device model, operating system and app version) through Google Firebase Crashlytics.

Usage statistics and crash reports never contain your questions, readings, name or birth details, and are not linked to your account identifier.

3.4 Advertising data (free users only)

If you do not have Premium and ads are switched on, Google AdMob, which provides the ads, may collect and process on your device: your advertising identifier, IP address, device and app information, and information about the ads shown and interacted with, to serve and measure ads, prevent fraud and, where you consent, personalize ads. Where the law requires it (for example in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland), Google shows a consent message before any ad is requested; you can reopen it in Settings → Privacy → Ad privacy choices. This choice is separate from the usage statistics choice above. If you have Premium, or ads are off, the ads software is not started and no ad data is collected. The app never sends AdMob your account identifier, name, birth details, questions or readings. Which readings you have unlocked with a rewarded ad is remembered only on your device.

3.5 Data we do not collect

We do not collect your precise device location, contacts or payment card details, and we do not store your photos (the advertising identifier is processed only by Google AdMob as described in 3.4, and not by us): the app never looks at your photo library except for the photos you choose, and photos used for a coffee reading are discarded once they have been read. When you search for your birth place, the search runs entirely on your device.

5. How readings are produced

Your birth chart and transits are calculated by our own astronomical software, and tarot cards are drawn by our servers with a secure random generator. Neither is done by artificial intelligence. The interpretation (readings, follow-up answers, daily guidance and reports) is written by Google's Gemini artificial intelligence service from your question, the cards drawn and calculated chart data. A coffee reading is written by Gemini from the photos of your cup and saucer, the optional topic and question, and the language of the app; it receives nothing else about you. Gemini never receives your name, birth date, birth place, account identifier or the other person's personal details in a compatibility report. We use Gemini under Google's paid service terms, under which Google does not use this content to improve its products. Texts written by artificial intelligence may be inaccurate; see our Terms of Use.

6. Who we share personal data with

We share personal data only with service providers that process it on our behalf and under our instructions, bound by data processing agreements:

  • Supabase (database, authentication and server functions; data stored in Frankfurt, Germany).
  • Google (Gemini API for writing readings, including reading the photos of a coffee reading; Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics, only with your consent).
  • Google AdMob (ads for free users, as described in 3.4). AdMob acts under Google's own terms and privacy policy (policies.google.com/privacy) and, for ads, is not limited to our instructions.
  • RevenueCat (processing and verifying purchases; it knows you only by your anonymous account identifier).

When you buy through Google Play, Google processes your payment as an independent controller under its own privacy policy. We may also disclose data if required by law, court order or a competent authority, to protect the rights, safety and property of our users or others, or as part of a merger, acquisition or sale of assets, in which case this policy will continue to apply to your data. We do not sell or rent personal data, and we do not share your questions, readings or birth details with advertisers.

7. International transfers

Our main database is in the European Union. Some service providers (Google and RevenueCat) may process data in other countries, including the United States. Where personal data is transferred outside the European Economic Area, the United Kingdom or Türkiye, we rely on an adequacy decision or on standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission and, for transfers from Türkiye, on the safeguards required by Article 9 of the KVKK, together with additional measures where needed.

9. Security

To protect the Service from abuse, the app uses Google Firebase App Check: Google Play (Play Integrity) confirms that requests come from a genuine copy of Hilora on a genuine device. This does not identify you and is not linked to your account.

We protect personal data with appropriate technical and organizational measures, including encryption in transit (TLS), access controls under which each account can read only its own data, storage of your session in your device's secure storage (Android Keystore), restricted administrative access, and logs that exclude the content of your questions and readings. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will notify you and the authorities of a personal data breach where the law requires.

10. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you have the right to:

  • be informed about and access the personal data we hold about you;
  • have inaccurate data corrected, and data deleted;
  • restrict or object to processing based on our legitimate interests;
  • receive your data in a portable format;
  • withdraw consent at any time, without affecting earlier processing;
  • under the KVKK, learn whether your data is processed, the purpose of processing, the third parties it is transferred to in Türkiye or abroad, ask for the transfer recipients to be notified of corrections or deletions, object to a result against you arising from analysis exclusively by automated systems, and claim compensation for damage caused by unlawful processing (KVKK Art. 11);
  • complain to a data protection authority, such as the authority in your country of residence in the EU, the UK Information Commissioner's Office, or the Turkish Personal Data Protection Authority (KVKK Board).

Many rights can be exercised directly in the app: edit your profile and birth details, delete readings and reports, change your usage data choice in Settings, or delete your account in Settings → Delete account. For other requests, email kadir.fayzi@gmail.com. Because accounts are anonymous, we may ask you for information that lets us identify your account, such as the store order number of a purchase. We answer within one month (30 days under the KVKK), free of charge unless a request is manifestly unfounded or excessive.

11. California residents

We do not sell personal information, and we do not use sensitive personal information for purposes other than providing the Service. Free users see ads served by Google AdMob, which may use the advertising identifier; you can reset or delete your advertising identifier and opt out of ads personalization in your device's Android settings (Settings → Google → Ads), and Premium users see no ads. California residents may request access to and deletion of their personal information as described in section 10, and will not be discriminated against for exercising these rights.

12. Children

The Service is intended only for adults aged 18 and over, and you must confirm your age before using it. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a minor has given us personal data, contact us and we will delete it.
