Delete your Hilora account

In the app

  1. Open Hilora and tap Settings in the tab bar.
  2. Tap Delete account and confirm.

Your account is deleted right away, together with everything stored with it: profile, birth details, chart, readings, daily guidance, reports, unused reading credits, and purchase and subscription records. This cannot be undone.

Without the app

If you no longer have the app, email privacy@hilora.app with the subject "Delete my account" and the App Store or Google Play order number of a Hilora purchase. Accounts are anonymous, so a purchase is the only way we can find yours without the app. We delete the account within 30 days and confirm by email.

Subscriptions

Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription. Cancel it in your App Store or Google Play subscription settings.

What we keep

Nothing linked to your account. Usage statistics and crash reports, if you allowed them, hold no account ID and expire on their own (2 months and 90 days). Apple and Google keep their own records of store purchases.
